This widget shows one row per monitor with a day-by-day history strip, so a service that was down
overnight is visible the next morning rather than only at the moment you look.

History is recorded once a day by the Uptime Sync task for Uptime Kuma monitors and for this
machine, and by the Ohmz AI and Plex probe tasks. The widget reads that history rather than polling
the services itself, so it is cheap to leave open.
